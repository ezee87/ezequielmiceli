## Research direction

Use current high-end interaction work as inspiration for:
- spatial editorial typography
- scroll-driven SVG storytelling
- subtle 2.5D depth
- progressive architecture construction
- shader-based media materialization
- restrained refractive surfaces

Do not copy an entire reference site's visual identity.

## Selection rule

When considering a component/effect from 21st.dev or React Bits:
1. Identify the strategic purpose.
2. Inspect the actual current source.
3. Adapt it to this design system.
4. Remove unnecessary decoration.
5. Verify mobile, reduced-motion and performance behavior.
6. Do not combine unrelated components just because they look impressive.
